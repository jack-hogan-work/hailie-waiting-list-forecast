# Project delivery summary

## Purpose

Help readers understand the likely direction of social housing waiting-list
counts in England and its nine regions, while showing the uncertainty around
those estimates.

## Published products

- `outputs/HAILIE_dashboard.html` — interactive national and regional outlook;
- `outputs/pdf/HAILIE_social_housing_waiting_list_briefing.pdf` — five-page
  briefing for media, policy and conference audiences;
- `outputs/HAILIE_final_report.html` — full evidence report; and
- `outputs/final/` — machine-readable results and reproducibility manifest.

## Quality standard

The data reconcile to the official published totals, model comparison is based
on forecasts of unseen historical years, uncertainty is shown alongside central
estimates, and every published output can be rebuilt from the repository.
