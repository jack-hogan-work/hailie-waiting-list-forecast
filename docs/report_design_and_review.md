# Publication design standards

The dashboard and briefing are designed around one public question: where could
social housing waiting-list counts be heading?

The publication standard is:

- lead with the answer and its uncertainty;
- use “social housing waiting list” consistently;
- keep technical evidence available without crowding the opening view;
- support England-wide and regional reading;
- use accessible contrast, semantic headings, visible keyboard focus and text
  labels that do not rely on colour alone;
- work on desktop and mobile screens; and
- distinguish observed counts, central forecasts and prediction ranges.

The final dashboard was checked at desktop and mobile sizes, and its geography
and forecast-horizon controls were tested before publication.
